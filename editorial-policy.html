Editorial Policy

A site that tells readers to distrust installer-funded calculators owes them a published account of its own standards. This is ours.

Independence

No advertiser, installer, manufacturer, distribution company or lender has any influence over content on this site. Specifically, we do not sell: a position in a comparison table, inclusion in or exclusion from a dataset, a rating, a review, or editorial control over research findings. Advertising is labelled. Sponsored content, where it exists, is labelled and never placed inside a calculator result or a comparison table.

The one genuine conflict of interest we carry is the referral fee on the optional quote form. We contain it by making the fee flat, identical across every installer, and invariant to which vendor you eventually buy from — and by publishing a price benchmark before the introduction, which makes the installer's job harder rather than easier. The full accounting is on our funding page.

Sourcing

  • Tariffs — State Electricity Regulatory Commission tariff orders and DISCOM published schedules.
  • Subsidy — the PM Surya Ghar national portal and Ministry of New and Renewable Energy notifications.
  • Solar yield — long-run state irradiance averages, cross-checked against modelled output.
  • Installed cost — real quotes submitted by readers and installers, aggregated into ranges rather than a single number.
  • EV efficiency — independent published road tests and owner-reported consumption, never manufacturer range claims.
  • Fuel prices — published city retail prices, which move frequently.

Where a figure is a range rather than a point, we publish the range. Where we are estimating, we say so.

How calculator assumptions are set

Assumptions are chosen to be conservative against the outcome we would benefit from. Our tariff escalation of 4% is below the historical Indian trend. We charge an inverter replacement to the model in year 12, which most published calculators omit. We credit exported units at 75% of retail rather than assuming a 1:1 settlement. Each of these makes solar look worse than a more generous model would, and each is in there deliberately.

Every assumption is listed on the relevant calculator page and is readable in the open calculation engine. If you think one is wrong, tell us and show your working — we have changed assumptions on reader challenge before.

Corrections

We treat a wrong number as a defect, not an embarrassment. Reported errors with a primary source attached are assessed the same day. Substantive corrections — anything that would change a reader's decision — are noted on the affected page with the date, rather than silently edited. We pay a bounty for accepted corrections; see contests.

Use of AI

We use AI tools for drafting assistance, code and data processing. We do not publish AI-generated figures, statistics or claims that a human has not verified against a primary source. Every number in a dataset on this site has been checked by a person against the document it came from. The failure mode of AI writing in this field is a plausible-sounding tariff rate that does not exist, and it is exactly the kind of error that costs a reader money — so verification is not optional here.

What we will not publish

  • A savings figure without the assumptions that produced it.
  • A "best of" ranking where inclusion was influenced by a commercial relationship.
  • Manufacturer range claims presented as real-world range.
  • A recommendation to install without stating the circumstances where it does not pay.
  • Traffic-chasing content on a topic we have not researched properly.

Bylines and expertise

Guides carry bylines. Our contributors are writers and analysts with backgrounds in energy, data and consumer finance. We do not claim engineering credentials we do not have — where a question is genuinely an engineering one, we say so and point you to a licensed professional.

Reader challenge

If you believe something here is wrong, biased or missing important context, write to us. We would rather be corrected by a reader than quoted by one while being wrong.
