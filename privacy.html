Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains what GridPe.com ("GridPe", "we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to this website and to any enquiry you send through it.

1. The short version

  • Using any calculator on this site sends nothing anywhere. The maths runs entirely in your browser.
  • We only receive personal information if you choose to fill in a form.
  • We do not sell your contact details to data brokers, aggregators or call-centre lists.
  • Third-party advertising and analytics may set cookies. You can control these.

2. Information you give us

When you submit a form — a solar quote request, a contact message, a job application, a contest entry or an advertising enquiry — we receive what you typed into it. Depending on the form, that may include your name, email address, phone number, PIN code, state, property type, indicative electricity bill band and any message you write.

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, your electricity consumer number, bank details, Aadhaar number, card details or passwords. GridPe has no legitimate reason to hold any of these.

3. Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising services we use may record standard technical information: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and timestamps. This is used to understand aggregate usage and to keep the site working, not to build a profile of you by name.

4. Local storage in your browser

We store a small amount of data in your own browser to remember your theme preference and whether you have dismissed the support banner. This never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

5. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry.
  • Where you have requested solar quotes, to pass your requirement to installers empanelled on the national portal who serve your PIN code — this is the entire purpose of that form and is the only circumstance in which we share your contact details with a third party.
  • To assess job applications and contest entries.
  • To improve the site's content and tools in aggregate.
  • To comply with a legal obligation.

6. Sharing — and what we never do

We share your contact details only with installers you have asked to be introduced to, and only those serving your area. Each introduction is limited: we do not distribute a single enquiry across a dozen vendors, we do not resell enquiries to lead aggregators, and we do not add you to any marketing list you did not ask for.

We may disclose information where required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights and safety of users. We may also share data with service providers who operate the site on our behalf — hosting, form relay, email — who are permitted to use it only to provide that service.

7. Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertising served by Google AdSense and other third-party vendors. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Third-party ad servers may also use cookies, JavaScript or web beacons in the advertisements appearing on this site, which are sent directly to your browser. GridPe has no access to or control over these cookies. Our policy does not govern the practices of third-party advertisers; please consult their own policies.

You can refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so does not prevent you from using any calculator on this site.

8. Video embeds

We use a click-to-load facade for video. Nothing is requested from YouTube, and no YouTube cookie is set, until you actively click play. When you do, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback. We use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain.

9. Retention

Enquiries are retained for as long as needed to handle them and to keep a record of the introduction, and are then deleted. Job applications are kept for twelve months unless you ask us to remove them sooner. Contest entries are kept for the duration of the contest and any subsequent prize administration.

10. Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct it if it is wrong, to delete it, or to stop using it for a particular purpose. Where you have given consent — for example to be contacted about a quote — you may withdraw it at any time, and we will stop. Write to us via the contact page and we will action the request within a reasonable period.

These rights reflect our obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where applicable to visitors from those jurisdictions, comparable protections elsewhere.

11. Children

This site is intended for adults making household financial decisions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are transmitted encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise — which is another reason we deliberately do not collect financial or identity documents.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The revision date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be noted on the page.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request relating to your data, go through the contact page.
