Where our money comes from — all of it

We think a site that tells you to distrust installer-funded calculators owes you a full accounting of its own incentives. Ours are:

  1. Display advertising. Programmatic ads served on content pages. Advertisers have no influence over editorial, no preview of articles, and no ability to buy a position in any table on this site.
  2. Lead referral. If you use the quote form, we may receive a referral fee from the installer we introduce you to. This is the clearest conflict of interest we have, so here is how we contain it: fees are flat per introduction, identical across every installer, and never varied by who you eventually buy from. No installer can pay to be listed first, and we publish the price benchmark before the introduction — which is precisely the information that makes an installer's job harder, not easier.
  3. Reader support. This page. No conditions attached and no influence purchased.
  4. Sponsorship. Clearly labelled, never inside a comparison or a data table.

We do not take payment for reviews, rankings, inclusion in any dataset, or "partner" placement in an article. If that ever changes, this paragraph changes with it.

Free things that help as much as money

  • Send a correction. A tariff that has moved, an EV spec that changed, a subsidy rule we have wrong. Corrections are the highest-value contribution to a data site. Tell us here.
  • Share a calculator with someone about to sign a solar contract. Most people get exactly one shot at that decision.
  • Send us your quotes. Anonymised real quotes are how we keep the ₹/W benchmark honest. Redact your name and send the numbers.
