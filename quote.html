Free · No obligation

Three real quotes. And a straight answer on whether to bother.

We match your requirement to installers empanelled on the national portal in your PIN code, and we send you the fair market ₹/W for your system size before anyone calls you. If rooftop solar does not make sense for your roof, we will tell you that instead of selling you one.

Empanelled vendors only

Installing through a vendor not registered on the portal makes your subsidy claim invalid. We only pass enquiries to registered ones.

Price benchmark first

You get the current market ₹/W range for your size, so you can tell an honest quote from a padded one.

One follow-up, then we stop

No resale to aggregator lists. No six-installers-calling-at-once. Say no and the file closes.

Subsidy paperwork help

The portal application, the documents that hold people up and the net-metering sequence — explained, free.

What happens after you submit

  1. We size the system from your bill and check the local yield for your state.
  2. You get a written benchmark: expected kW, fair ₹/W band, subsidy amount and realistic payback.
  3. We introduce up to three empanelled installers who actually serve your PIN code.
  4. You compare like for like — we give you the checklist of what to compare.
What we ask you to compare, not just the price

Module brand and wattage, whether cells are DCR (required for subsidy), inverter brand and warranty term, structure height and galvanisation, cable gauge, whether the net-meter application and liaison are included, the performance warranty, and the AMC terms after year one. Two quotes that differ by ₹40,000 usually differ on three of these — the cheaper one is not always worse, but it is never identical.
