Contest terms

Organiser. GridPe (GridPe.com), an independent digital publisher. All contests are administered by the organiser and are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, any social media platform, search engine or government body.

Nature of the contest. All GridPe contests are skill-based competitions. Entries are assessed on merit against the published judging criteria by the GridPe editorial desk. No element of chance determines the outcome, and there is no lottery, lucky draw or random selection at any stage.

No purchase necessary. Entry is free. No purchase, subscription or payment of any kind is required to enter or to win, and making one does not improve your chances.

Eligibility. Open to residents of India aged 18 or over. Employees of the organiser, their immediate families, and anyone with a commercial relationship with the organiser are not eligible. Entries from jurisdictions where such competitions are restricted by local law will be disqualified without prejudice; it is the entrant's responsibility to confirm eligibility in their own state.

Entry period. Each contest's window is stated on its card above, in Indian Standard Time. Entries received outside the window are not considered.

Judging. Entries are scored against the criteria named on each contest card. Where evidence cannot be verified, the entry is excluded. The judges' decision is final and no correspondence will be entered into regarding individual scores.

Prizes. Prizes are as stated, paid in Indian rupees by bank transfer or UPI to an account in the winner's own name. Prizes are not transferable and there is no cash alternative beyond the stated cash prize. Where the value of a prize exceeds ₹10,000, tax is deducted at source under section 194B of the Income-tax Act, 1961 and the winner is responsible for any further tax liability.

Winner announcement. Winners are notified by email within fourteen days of the contest closing and published on this page by first name and city only. A winner who does not respond within fourteen days of notification forfeits the prize.

Use of entries. By entering you grant GridPe a non-exclusive right to publish your entry, in whole or in part and in anonymised form, in editorial content. You retain ownership of your submission. Personal data is handled in accordance with our privacy policy and is not shared with advertisers or installers.

Cancellation. The organiser may amend, suspend or cancel a contest where circumstances outside its reasonable control make it necessary, and will publish notice of any such change on this page.
