Disclaimer

Last updated: 2026

The one-line version

Everything on this site is an estimate built from published data and stated assumptions. Verify it against your own bill and a written quotation before you spend money.

Not professional advice

GridPe publishes general information for an Indian audience. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax, legal or engineering advice, and nothing here takes account of your particular circumstances. A rooftop solar installation is an electrical and structural intervention in your building; an EV purchase is a significant financial commitment. Both warrant advice from a qualified professional who has seen your specific situation.

What our estimates assume

Our solar model assumes an unshaded, well-oriented array; state-average irradiance; 0.6% annual panel degradation; 4% annual tariff escalation; ₹500 per kW per year maintenance; an inverter replacement in year 12; exported units credited at 75% of the retail tariff; and an 8% discount rate for net present value. Our EV figures assume 88% AC and 94% DC charging efficiency against independently tested real-world consumption. Our bill figures assume a domestic LT connection under the slab structure published in the relevant tariff order.

Change any of those assumptions and the answer changes. That is why we publish them rather than hiding them.

Data may be out of date

State commissions revise tariffs annually. Fuel surcharge components move more often, sometimes monthly. Subsidy rules, net-metering regulations and vehicle specifications all change without notice to us. Every dataset on this site carries an "as of" date. If that date is not recent, treat the figures with proportionate caution — and please tell us.

No guarantee of savings

We make no representation that you will achieve any particular saving, payback period, generation figure or return. Actual outcomes depend on roof conditions, shading, installation quality, equipment selection, maintenance, consumption behaviour, tariff changes and policy decisions outside anyone's control.

Electrical safety

Do not attempt rooftop solar installation, meter work, consumer-unit modification or EV charger wiring yourself. This is licensed work in India for good reason. Solar arrays carry lethal DC voltage even when the grid supply is off.
