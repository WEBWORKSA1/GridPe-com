About GridPe

GridPe — "Grid Pe", on the grid — exists because the most expensive energy decisions Indian households make are being made on the basis of numbers supplied by the people selling the equipment.

The problem we are solving

Roughly one crore Indian households are expected to install rooftop solar under PM Surya Ghar. Each one is a decision of ₹1 lakh to ₹3 lakh, made once, usually by someone with no prior exposure to per-watt pricing, net-metering rules, module certification or discounted cash flow. The information available to them at the point of decision comes overwhelmingly from installers.

The same pattern repeats with electric vehicles, where certified range overstates real range by 20–35%, and with electricity bills, where most people cannot say what their effective rate per unit actually is — the single number that determines whether any of this pays.

GridPe's answer is narrow and specific: build the calculators that give a rupee answer in under a minute, show every assumption, and take no money from anyone who benefits from the answer being optimistic.

How we work

  • Result first, contact never required. Every calculator returns the full answer without a form. We regard gating a number behind a phone number as a tell that the number is not the point.
  • Assumptions published. Degradation, escalation, maintenance, inverter replacement, export credit and discount rate are all stated on the page and visible in the calculation engine. Disagree with one, change it, see what happens.
  • Primary sources. Tariffs come from State Electricity Regulatory Commission orders. Subsidy structure comes from the national portal. EV efficiency comes from independent road tests, not manufacturer claims.
  • Dated data. Every dataset carries a version and an "as of" stamp. Stale data is a defect and we would rather you could see it.
  • Downside stated. Our solar calculator has a section on when rooftop solar does not pay. Our EV calculator says plainly that the ₹1/km claim only holds under specific conditions. This costs us conversions and we consider that the point.

How we make money

Display advertising, referral fees on the optional quote form, reader support and clearly-labelled sponsorship. The full accounting — including exactly how we contain the conflict of interest in the referral model — is on our funding page, which we would encourage you to read before trusting anything else here.

What we do not sell: position in a comparison table, inclusion in a dataset, favourable ratings, or editorial control over research findings. No installer, manufacturer, DISCOM or lender has any influence over the figures on this site.

Who writes this

GridPe is produced by a small remote research desk of writers and data analysts working across India. Bylines appear on guides. We are actively hiring analysts who can read a tariff order and tell us what is wrong with it.

What we are not

We are not a government body, not affiliated with PM Surya Ghar or any ministry, not a DISCOM, not a solar installer, not a lender and not a financial adviser. We cannot access your electricity account, process your subsidy application or resolve a billing dispute. For any of those, your distribution company is the correct destination — the directory lists every one.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, we want to know quickly, and we pay a bounty for corrections backed by a primary source. Send it through. Substantive corrections are noted on the affected page rather than quietly edited.
