Work with us

GridPe is a small, remote team building the most trustworthy energy-cost resource in India. We care more about whether you can read a tariff order and spot what is wrong with it than about where you went to college.

Energy Writer / Analyst

Open

Remote (India) · Project-based or full-time · ₹40,000–90,000 per month equivalent

Write the guides that decide ₹1.5 lakh purchases. You will read SERC tariff orders, track PM Surya Ghar changes, interview installers and turn it into writing that a first-time buyer can act on.

Must have

  • Demonstrable ability to read a regulatory document and extract what matters
  • Writing samples where you explained something numerical to a non-expert
  • Comfort saying "this does not pay" when it does not

Nice to have

  • Background in power, renewables or personal finance journalism
  • Working Hindi or a second Indian language

Data Analyst — Tariffs & EV

Open

Remote · Part-time or contract · ₹30,000–70,000 per month equivalent

Own the datasets behind every calculator. You will maintain slab structures for 35+ DISCOMs, track fuel-surcharge revisions and keep EV efficiency figures honest against independent test data.

Must have

  • Python or JavaScript, and genuine care about data structure
  • Patience for primary sources — PDFs, tariff orders, government portals
  • An instinct for when a number looks wrong
