How to read these tables

Telescopic means each slab rate applies only to the units inside that slab — consuming 250 units bills the first 100 at the lowest rate and the next 150 higher. Flat means the slab your total lands in sets one rate for everything you consumed; crossing a boundary can raise the rate on your entire consumption at once.

Fixed charge is levied per kilowatt of sanctioned load per month in most states, and as a flat monthly minimum in others. You pay it whether or not you consume anything.

Fuel and power purchase cost adjustment (FPPCA, FAC or PPAC depending on the state) is a pass-through of the DISCOM's actual power procurement cost, charged as a percentage of the energy charge. It is the most volatile line on any Indian electricity bill.

Electricity duty is a state tax on the energy and fixed charges combined. It ranges from nil to 16%.

Free-unit schemes

Several states now waive part of the bill for eligible domestic consumers — Delhi up to 200 units, Karnataka's Gruha Jyoti, Telangana's Gruha Jyothi, Punjab's 300 units, Tamil Nadu's first 100 units per two-month cycle, Jharkhand's 200 units. These are applied after the tariff calculation and almost always require opting in through the DISCOM. They are also policy, not physics: they can be revised or withdrawn, which is worth remembering when you are evaluating a 25-year solar investment against a currently-zero bill.
